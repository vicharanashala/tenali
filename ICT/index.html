Intersecting Lines

🛣️ Analogy: Two Crossroads Meeting

Different slopes mean the lines tilt differently and meet at exactly one point (x, y). Since a solution exists, the pair is Consistent.

Inconsistent • No Solution 02

Parallel Lines

🚆 Analogy: Straight Train Tracks

Identical slopes keep the lines equally spaced forever. They never cross, leaving zero common points—making the system Inconsistent.

a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂

Coincident Lines

🚗 Analogy: Two Cars, Same Exact Path

One equation is a scaled multiple of the other. Every point lies on both lines—giving infinitely many solutions (Consistent & Dependent).
