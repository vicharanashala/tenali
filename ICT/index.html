THE BIG IDEA

What does it mean
to balance an equation?

A chemical reaction rearranges atoms. It does not create or destroy them. A balanced equation has the same number of each type of atom on both sides.

01

Count the atoms

Find how many atoms of each element appear in the reactants and products.

02

Change coefficients

Place whole-number coefficients in front of formulas. Never change subscripts.

03

Check both sides

Count again. Each element must have equal totals on the left and right.

⚖
The law of conservation of mass

Matter is conserved in a chemical reaction. Balancing equations is how we show this using symbols.

Mass in = Mass out
