01 / THE CONCEPT

What is evaporation?

Evaporation is a natural process that happens all around us.

💧 → ☁️

Liquid changes into vapour

Evaporation is the process by which a liquid changes into vapour from its surface at temperatures below its boiling point. It is a surface phenomenon and can occur at any temperature.

🌡️

Can happen at any temperature

Evaporation can take place even when a liquid is not boiling.

🫧

Surface phenomenon

Only molecules at the exposed surface can escape directly into the air.

❄️

Cooling effect

Higher-energy molecules escape, reducing the average energy of the remaining liquid.

02 / WHAT CHANGES THE RATE?

Factors affecting evaporation

The rate of evaporation depends on several conditions.

☀️

Temperature

Higher temperature gives particles more kinetic energy, so evaporation becomes faster.

Higher temperature ↑ rate
💨

Wind speed

Moving air carries away water vapour near the surface, allowing more liquid to evaporate.

Higher wind speed ↑ rate
🫗

Surface area

A larger exposed surface allows more particles to escape at the same time.

Larger surface area ↑ rate
💦

Humidity

When air already contains a lot of water vapour, evaporation becomes slower.

Higher humidity ↓ rate

03 / SCIENCE AROUND US

Evaporation in everyday life

We observe evaporation in many familiar situations.

👕

Drying of wet clothes

Water in wet clothes evaporates into the surrounding air.

🏃

Cooling by sweating

Evaporation of sweat removes heat from our skin and cools the body.

🌦️

Drying of puddles

After rain, water on roads and other surfaces gradually evaporates.

🌸

Perfume spreading

Volatile liquid components evaporate and their vapour spreads through air.
