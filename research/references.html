🇮🇳

National — India

📋

Government Reports & Policy Documents

  • N01
    "National Education Policy 2020" — Ministry of Education, Government of India (2020)
    India's landmark new education policy declaring FLN a national priority. Explicitly notes children who don't achieve FLN by Grade 3 rarely catch up.
    www.education.gov.in/en/nep
  • N02
    "National Curriculum Framework for Foundational Stage (NCF-FS) 2022" — NCERT (2022)
    Detailed pedagogical guidelines for ages 3–8 (foundational stage), with play-based learning, CRA approach, and activity-based numeracy. Operational curriculum for NIPUN Bharat.
    ncf.ncert.gov.in
  • N03
    "NIPUN Bharat Mission" — Ministry of Education, Government of India (2021)
    India's flagship national FLN mission targeting 25 crore children with a goal of FLN for all by end of Grade 3 by 2026–27.
    nipunbharat.education.gov.in
  • N04
    "National Achievement Survey (NAS) 2021" — Ministry of Education (2021)
    3.8 million students, 1.18 lakh schools. Grade 3 averages: 59% language, 57% math. Most cited data point in NEP 2020's FLN justification.
    nas.gov.in/report-card/nas-2021
  • N05
    "NCF 2023 (National Curriculum Framework)" — NCERT, Government of India (2023)
    NCF 2023 extends the curricular framework beyond foundational stage to secondary stage (ages 14–18). Important for understanding Tenali's position in the broader curriculum.
    ncf.ncert.gov.in
  • N06
    "PARAKH — Performance Assessment, Review and Analysis of Knowledge for Holistic Development" — Ministry of Education, India
    India's national assessment regulatory body. PARAKH's 2025 FLN assessment results represent the first formal national FLN measurement in India.
    nas.gov.in (PARAKH results)

Research Papers

  • N07
    "Foundational Literacy and Numeracy Under NEP 2020: A Pathway to Holistic Education" — IJFMR (2025)
    Academic paper examining FLN significance, implementation challenges, and policy measures under NEP 2020.
    www.ijfmr.com/papers/2025/2/40653.pdf
  • N08
    "Foundational Literacy and Numeracy in India: Challenges, Policies and the Path Forward" — Khritish Swargiary, ResearchGate (2024)
    Peer-reviewed research examining current state of FLN in India, focusing on challenges and effectiveness of policy measures.
    researchgate.net/publication/383942536
  • N09
    "The Success of the NIPUN Bharat Scheme: A Case Study" — IJFMR (2025)
    Qualitative case study analyzing NIPUN Bharat mission implementation across Uttar Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka.
    www.ijfmr.com/papers/2025/3/47345.pdf
  • N10
    "Bridging Foundational Learning Gaps: Poverty, Health, Social Barriers" — IMPRI India (2025)
    Analysis of structural obstacles to FLN: poverty, malnutrition, centralized implementation patterns, and digital gaps.
    impriindia.com/insights/strategies-in-the-nipun-bharat-mission
  • N11
    "Enhancing Foundational Literacy and Numeracy in India: Key Strategies and Insights" — iDreamEducation (2024)
    Comprehensive analysis of FLN strategies in India synthesizing NIPUN Bharat data, ASER reports, and NAS data.
    www.idreameducation.org/blog/foundational-literacy-numeracy-fln-india

Reports & Assessments

  • N12
    "ASER 2024: Full Report" — ASER Centre / Pratham Education Foundation (2024)
    India's most comprehensive citizen-led household learning assessment. 600,000+ children assessed annually. Most authoritative FLN data source.
    ASER 2024 Full Report (PDF)
  • N13
    "ASER 2024: National Findings" — ASER Centre / Pratham (2024)
    Summary findings: ~40% of Class 3 students cannot read Class 1 text; significant rural-urban and regional gaps.
    ASER 2024 National Findings (PDF)
  • N14
    "Tracking Regional Disparities in Learning Outcomes" — ORF / PARAKH (2025)
    Analysis of PARAKH 2025 national FLN assessment results revealing significant regional disparities and confirming the extended FLN gap.
    orfonline.org/...regional-disparities
  • N15
    "India's Learning Crisis: Are Children in School But Not Learning?" — Education For All in India (2024/25)
    Evidence-based analysis of India's learning crisis drawing on ASER, UDISE, and NAS 2021 data with policy action agenda.
    educationforallinindia.com (PDF)
  • N16
    "CSF's Approach to System Reform for Foundational Literacy and Numeracy" — Central Square Foundation (2023/2024)
    India-focused education reform NGO's comprehensive FLN system reform framework, synthesizing implementation evidence from Indian states.
    centralsquarefoundation.org/...fln
  • N17
    "Reinventing Foundational Learning: FLN in India" — Smile Foundation India (2024)
    Indian NGO perspective on FLN challenges and path forward, with field-level implementation insights.
    smilefoundationindia.org/blog/reinventing-foundations-fln-in-india

Newspaper Articles

  • N18
    "ASER 2024 Key Insights — India's Learning Report Card" — The Hindu (2024)
    The Hindu's analysis of ASER 2024 findings: big worries in literacy and numeracy skills, educational disparities, and need for innovative assessment methods.
    thehindu.com/...article69158986.ece

International

🏛️

Government Reports & Policy Documents

  • I01
    "Ending Learning Poverty — What it will take to eliminate learning poverty by 2030" — World Bank Group (2023)
    World Bank's flagship FLN document. $2B+ financing commitment, evidence base, and country program guidance. India is the single largest contributor to global learning poverty.
    worldbank.org/...ending-learning-poverty
  • I02
    "UNESCO Global Education Monitoring Report" — UNESCO (annual)
    UNESCO's annual GEM reports are the world's most comprehensive education monitoring instruments, regularly featuring FLN, learning poverty, and India's progress.
    unesco.org/...global-education-monitoring-report
  • I03
    "UNESCO — Education for All / SDG 4" — UNESCO
    UNESCO's global education monitoring and advocacy for SDG 4 (Quality Education). Regular reports, funding guidance, and normative frameworks for FLN.
    unesco.org/education/education-for-all
  • I04
    "Foundational Learning Action Tracker 2025: Global Report" — UNICEF (2025)
    UNICEF's comprehensive 2025 global FLN report tracking what works in foundational learning across 150+ countries.
    UNICEF FLN Report 2025 (PDF)
  • I05
    "UNICEF FLN Hub — Resources for Foundational Literacy and Numeracy" — UNICEF (2024)
    UNICEF's dedicated FLN platform: implementation guides, research syntheses, evidence briefs, and country program resources. India's NIPUN Bharat is a central case study.
    unicef.org/flnhub
  • I06
    "OECD — Programme for International Student Assessment (PISA)" — OECD
    Internationally comparable data on learning outcomes for 15-year-olds across 90+ countries. India's participation (2009, 2022) showed significant learning gaps vs. global averages.
    oecd.org/pisa

Research Papers

  • I07
    "Exploring Behavioral Patterns and Predicting Academic Success: Insights from ML in Adaptive Mathematics Learning Systems" — Springer, Lecture Notes in Social Networks (2025)
    Academic paper on using ML to predict academic success in adaptive math learning systems — directly relevant to Tenali's adaptive regression engine.
    link.springer.com/...978-3-031-99997-0_15
  • I08
    "Beyond Static Question Banks: Dynamic Knowledge Expansion via LLM-Automated Graph Construction and Adaptive Generation" — arXiv:2602.00020 (2026)
    Pre-print on using LLMs to dynamically construct knowledge graphs and generate adaptive questions — directly relevant to Tenali's question bank architecture.
    doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.00020
  • I09
    "The design and implementation of an educational Chatbot with personalized adaptive learning features" — Chen et al., Tech. Know. Learn. (2024)
    Research on chatbot-based adaptive learning systems with personalized paths — relevant to Tenali's story-intro delivery mechanism.
    doi.org/10.1007/s10758-024-09807-5
  • I10
    "Adaptive learning models for diverse classrooms: enhancing educational equity" — Dagunduro et al., IJARSS (2024)
    Study on adaptive models for diverse classrooms with equity focus — directly relevant to Tenali's FLN audience.
    doi.org/10.51594/ijarss.v6i9.1588
  • I11
    "Student Modeling and Analysis in Adaptive Instructional Systems" — IEEE Xplore (2022)
    IEEE paper on student modeling techniques in adaptive instructional systems — foundational for understanding learner state tracking.
    ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9784853
  • I12
    "Predictive Models for Educational Purposes: A Systematic Review" — MDPI Data (2024)
    Systematic review of ML/DM models used to predict student performance in educational settings — comprehensive survey of techniques.
    mdpi.com/2504-2289/8/12/187
  • I13
    "Predicting student performance in online learning platforms: Analyzing engagement metrics with ML models" — ResearchGate (2024)
    ML-based prediction of student performance from engagement data — relevant to identifying struggle indicators and dropout risk.
    researchgate.net/publication/385541072
  • I14
    "Cognitive Load Theory" — Sweller (1988/1989), Journal of Educational Psychology
    Foundational paper by John Sweller establishing Cognitive Load Theory (CLT). The theoretical basis for why regression-based adaptive systems work.
    sciencedirect.com/.../0362161288900296
  • I15
    "J-PAL (Abhijit Banerjee, Esther Duflo) — Improving learning outcomes through RCTs" — MIT J-PAL
    Hundreds of RCTs on education interventions in developing countries. Their "Improving Learning Outcomes" review is essential FLN evidence.
    povertyactionlab.org
  • I16
    "RISE — Research on Improving Systems of Education" — RISE Programme (2016–present)
    Multi-country research initiative in India, Ethiopia, Pakistan, and Tanzania. India's findings on ABL effectiveness are foundational to NEP 2020's FLN approach.
    riseeducation.org
  • I17
    "The Learning Lab (USA) — Early Literacy Programs Review" — The Learning Lab (2010–present)
    Systematic reviews identifying high-impact, cost-effective early literacy interventions (systematic phonics, guided reading, interactive read-alouds). Directly informs FLN pedagogy.
    learninglab.org
