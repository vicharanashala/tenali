    🍎 Apple free-fall controls

    Choose a height and drop the apple to calculate fall time and impact speed.

    Free-fall animation · Selected world

    🍎

    Physics equations

    Fall time: t = √(2h/g)

    Impact speed: v = √(2gh)

    Weight of apple: W = mg

    Ideal free fall assumes the apple starts from rest and ignores air resistance.

    Launch the projectile, then press “Pause at instant” to inspect its position and velocity components.

    Physics equations

    Horizontal velocity: vₓ = u cos θ

    Vertical velocity: vᵧ = u sin θ − gt

    Horizontal position: x = u cos θ · t

    Vertical position: y = h + u sin θ · t − ½gt²

    The horizontal acceleration is zero; vertical acceleration is −g.

    ❓ HOW TO PLAY

    • Fly around: drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom. Use the tabs on top to jump between the Universe, our Galaxy, famous Stars and the Solar System.
    • Visit a world: click any object (or its name label / button at the bottom). The camera flies to it and a welcome page opens — with a voice greeting.
    • Try a jump: on planets and moons, see the surface gravity and press the jump button to animate a person. The estimated height assumes the same take-off effort as a 0.5 m jump on Earth.
    • Measure its mass: open the Mass Lab, optionally predict the mass, launch an orbiting probe, time one lap and let Kepler's law reveal the mass.
    • Earn XP: every new discovery and accurate measurement earns XP. Finish missions to rank up from Cadet to Galaxy Master.
    • Keys: Space pause · L labels · M mute voice · Esc close page.
